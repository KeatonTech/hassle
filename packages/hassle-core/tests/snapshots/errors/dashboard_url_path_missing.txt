`@dashboard(...)` at bad.py:6 declares neither `url_path=` nor `default=True`. Every dashboard is identified either by its HA `url_path` (the slug in its sidebar link) or by being THE default dashboard, and Hassle never guesses which one you meant -- guessing would silently overwrite the default dashboard. Fix: pass `url_path="my-dashboard"` for a sidebar dashboard, or `default=True` for the one HA serves at `/lovelace`.
