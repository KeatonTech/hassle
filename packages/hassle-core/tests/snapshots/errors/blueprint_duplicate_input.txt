Two different `bp_input('light')` declarations are both reachable from the blueprint `automation/local/one.yaml` -- one at shared.py:3, the other at shared.py:4. HA's `blueprint.input` block is a mapping, so one would silently replace the other along with its selector and default, and which one survived would depend on declaration order rather than on anything you wrote. Fix: delete one of them and reference the surviving `InputRef` from both places, or give one of them a different name. Two declarations sharing a name is only a problem when a single blueprint can see both -- separate blueprints each get their own `input:` namespace and may reuse the name freely.
