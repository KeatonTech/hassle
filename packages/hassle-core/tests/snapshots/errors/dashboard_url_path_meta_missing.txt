The `@raw_dashboard` body at bad.py:11 returned a `meta` dict with no `url_path` key. `meta` IS the HA dashboard-registry item, and its `url_path` is the dashboard's identity -- without it the envelope would fall through to the `default` identity sentinel and silently overwrite the default dashboard.
